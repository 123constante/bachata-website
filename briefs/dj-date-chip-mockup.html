The DJ date on the wall

The full date "Friday 23 October" no longer fits on one line of a wall tile, so it wraps to two rows and covers about 40% of the face. Below are three ways to fix it, drawn with the wall's real sizes and colours and the first 12 DJs on /djs today. Each option is shown at 360 and 390 wide. The person page (hub) keeps the full "Friday 23 October" in every option.

A Short date on the tile

The tile chip says "Fri 23 Oct" on one line. The full date stays on the person page.

  • Face stays clear; tiles stay the same height as before.
  • Breaks your copy rule "Friday, not Fri". It would be the one place on the site with a short day name.

B Date under the name

No chip on the face. "Friday 23 October" sits in gold under the name, full words.

  • Keeps your full-weekday rule; the face is fully visible.
  • The date wraps to two short lines under the name, so DJ tiles get taller and the wall is a little longer to scroll.

C Keep it as it is now

The chip on the face with the full date, wrapping to two rows. This is what #963 does today.

  • No change needed.
  • Covers the lower part of the face (initials or photo) on every DJ tile with a gig.

Drawn from the tokens in src/modules/person-page/wall/tokens.ts on PR #963. Photos are shown as initials here (this page cannot load the site's images); flags are approximations.
