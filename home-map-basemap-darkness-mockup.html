Real Esri tiles, London z14, on the live #11121a page background. Card height matches the mobile map card (216px at a 390px viewport). Labels are never filtered — only the base layer is.

Why the shipped one looks black. It matches CARTO's average brightness almost exactly (18.0 vs 18.9) but has half the contrast range (spread 21.7 vs 40), and its darkest pixels never reach black (p05 = 5.4, where CARTO's was 2). CARTO was a near-black background with bright roads; the shipped filter compresses everything into one narrow grey band, so there is no structure to see. Raising contrast, not brightness, is what brings it back.
