Approach C

Flagship event

The hero is compressed; immediately below sits a "Next event" billboard with poster gradient, date, name, venue, and a CTA. A compact identity ribbon follows. Subsequent upcoming events sit as a 3-col compact secondary grid.

Above-fold honesty: on a 375×667 mobile, the user sees breadcrumb + compressed hero + the full flagship billboard before the fold. The identity ribbon (with avatar, since-year pill and 3 social icons) sits at ≈530px — just visible. Trade-off: identity is de-prioritised: the visitor sees the next event before they see who runs it. Strongest pull-through to ticket sales / attendance, weakest signal of organiser identity. Works well only when the next event has a strong poster / clear name. Fails gracefully if no upcoming event (the billboard hides; ribbon promotes itself).
