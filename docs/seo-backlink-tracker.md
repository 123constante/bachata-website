# SEO Backlink Tracker — Bachata Calendar

**Goal:** ~15&ndash;30 quality referring domains within 90 days. Backlinks are ~24% of
organic ranking weight and the slowest-compounding lever, so start now.

**How to use:** work top-down (warm contacts first). Fill in `Asked` / `Live` dates
and the linking `URL` as you go. Followed links are best; a nofollow from a big
site still earns brand value.

**Link target rule:** organisers/venues link to *their own* `/organisers/{slug}` or
`/venue-entity/{slug}` page where it exists and is indexable; otherwise to the
homepage (`https://www.bachatacalendar.co.uk/`) or `/london-bachata-guide`. Vary the
anchor text naturally ("Bachata Calendar", "London's bachata calendar", "see all
London bachata events") &mdash; never "click here".

> Note: organiser **detail** pages are currently feature-gated (noindex in prod).
> Until Phase 1.5 unlocks `VITE_ENABLE_ORGANISER_DETAIL`, point organiser outreach
> at the homepage or guide. After unlock, switch the target to their profile page.
