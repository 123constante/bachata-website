## VERDICT: BLOCK

Findings 1 and 2 are blockers. Once the backfill runs, rollback no longer works, and the D4 plan for `cover_hash` needs SQL and breaks the match. Both have contract-only fixes (above). After that, the contract can pass with fixes 3-11 and 17.
