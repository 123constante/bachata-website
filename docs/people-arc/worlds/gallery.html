People pages: the three worlds

P2b mockups, 2026-10-23. Red frame = today’s live page (P0 audit shots), green frame = the proposal. Header and footer in every AFTER are the live site’s own pixels. Photos are a striped stand-in (the image host is unreachable from the build sandbox). Data: prod read-only, see data.md.

What is the same on every world

  • Back (left) and Share (right) in one bar, directly under the site header, at the same height on every page.
  • The role tabs sit under that bar and above the photo, the same component in the same place (y = 390:164, 360:164 px). They list ALL the person’s roles, the current one filled in its role colour; tapping goes to /teachers|/djs|/dancers/<slug>. One-role people get no tab row.
  • One placeholder family: a circle with initials, coloured by the world. One date format (“Thursday 29 October”), counts only from 2, languages as full names, nationality as a flag, city without a postcode.
  • Names: the DJ world shows the DJ name big and the public name small under it (Morcilla / Papacito); when there is no DJ name (Ramiro) only the public name shows. Dancer and teacher worlds show the public name.
  • No “coming soon” blocks, no empty cards: a section only appears when it has rows. The footer comes up right after the content.
