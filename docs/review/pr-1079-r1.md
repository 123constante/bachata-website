### Findings, ranked

**F1 (should-fix, CONFIRMED). A late save success closes a DIFFERENT add/edit sheet and wipes the draft typed into it.**
`TypeSheet.tsx:108-112` calls `onSaved(...)` after the await with no `alive` check (the refusal path at `:62` does check it). The parent's `onSaved` at `TicketsEntry.tsx:315-317` does `setEditing(null); setDraft(null)` unconditionally.
Scenario (slow network): tap Put on sale, Escape (sheet closes, draft dropped, correct), tap Add a ticket again, type "Second ticket". The first save lands: the second sheet vanishes, "Second ticket" is gone, toast "Ticket added and on sale." The person has lost a draft and may think the SECOND ticket was added. Same path for an edit closed mid-save and re-opened.
Probe (appended to a copy of `stack.draft.test.tsx`, then deleted): after `answer({ ok: true })`, `layers()` is `['tix-tall']`, expected `['tix-tall', 'tix-sheet']`. FAILS on head.
Fix shape: the sheet reports a late success through a separate `onLateSaved(text)` (toast + refresh only), or `TicketsEntry` keys `editing`/`draft` by the sheet instance that started the write and ignores a stale one. Add the probe as a test.

**F2 (should-fix, CONFIRMED, money). A late refund success for order A closes the Refund sheet now open for order B.**
`RefundSheet.tsx:49` `if (out.kind === 'ok') return onDone('Refund started.')` with no `alive` check (the refusal at `:51` has one). `TicketsEntry.tsx:335-338` `onDone` does `setRefunding(null)` whatever sheet is open. The in-flight lock (`inFlight`) only disables order A's button, so order B's Refund is reachable.
Scenario: Refund A, confirm, Keep the order (A's row says "Refunding..."), tap Refund on B. A's refund lands: B's sheet disappears under a toast "Refund started." The person reads that as B refunded; B is not, and A's row (now refreshed) is the one that changed. For a money action this is the wrong message at the wrong moment.
Probe: after `answer({ kind: 'ok' })`, `layers()` is `['tix-tall']`, expected `['tix-tall', 'tix-refund-sheet']`. FAILS on head.
Fix shape: as F1 (a late success toasts and refreshes but never touches `refunding`), or lock EVERY Refund button while any refund is in flight and say so.

**F3 (should-fix, a11y of a money refusal). Late refusals are announced politely and vanish in 8 s.**
`TicketsEntry.tsx:347-353`: `TicketToast` is `role="status"`, no dismiss, 8 s timer. A late refund or save refusal (`onLate`) is routed into it (`:314`, `:332`). In-sheet the same refusal is `role="alert"` and takes focus (`RefundSheet.tsx:42,78`). A screen-reader user who closed the sheet never hears "Payments are not switched on yet..." as an alert; a sighted user who looked away for 8 s never learns the refund did not happen, and A's Refund button is simply back. Fix: `role="alert"` for refusals and no auto-dismiss for them (or a Dismiss control, 44 px).

**F4 (should-fix, round-1 regression). One switch success clears every other row's error.**
`TicketsEntry.tsx:142` `setMoveError(new Map())` on success. Round 1 made errors per row (`:92`). Scenario: switch on paid type A is refused ("Connect payments in You > Get paid..."), switch on free type B succeeds: A's reason disappears while A is still Not on sale, with no explanation under it. Fix: only delete `t.id` (`with_(m, null)` is already there at `:137`).

**F5 (nit). Price lock can flip under the person's fingers.**
`TicketsEntry.tsx:308` `hasOrdersOf(editing.type, sales, orders.data ?? [])` is false until the summary and orders land; `TypeSheet.tsx:230` then disables the price input mid-edit. Usually cached (30 s stale) so rare; the server still refuses, so no bad write. Say "Checking sales..." while `summary.isPending`, or open the editor only once both reads are known.

**F6 (nit). Escape inside the "Close sales for good?" confirm closes the whole edit sheet.**
`TypeSheet.tsx:302-311`: `confirmClose` is sheet-local; Escape goes to `StackSheet` (`:77-81`) and closes the layer. Expected by most people: Escape = "Keep selling". Harmless (nothing is sent) but surprising.

## Part 2: Design Gate stage 5 critique against mockup C

Method: the brief's impeccable-finish-reviewer approach, by reading the TSX and the appended CSS against `mockups/p3-wt-ticketing/C-tickets-sheet.html` (no browser; the subagent that did the first pass could not walk either). ### Findings, ranked

**D1 (should-fix, CONFIRMED by specificity). You > Get paid row: title and subtitle render as one run-on line.**
`GetPaid.tsx:190-191` stacks `Get paid` (`p3-row-t p3-block`) over `Stripe pays ticket money into your bank` (`p3-row-s`) and relies on `p3-block`. But `p3.css:157` `.p3-theme .p3-status-row .p3-row-t, ... .p3-row-s { display: inline }` (three classes) beats `p3.css:236` `.p3-theme .p3-block { display: block }` (two classes); JSX drops the whitespace between the spans. Rendered: "**Get paid**Stripe pays ticket money into your bank", a bold word glued to muted text, wrapping mid-sentence before the tag and chevron. Mockup: `.t` is `display: block`, two lines. The Tickets row avoids it only because it puts a literal `" \u00B7 "` in its subtitle (`TicketsEntry.tsx:272`). Not caught by `getPaid.matrix.test.tsx:137-147` (tag and href only). Fix: a more specific rule (`.p3-status-row .p3-block`), or the same `" \u00B7 "` separator as the Tickets row.

**D2 (should-fix, density). Ticket type rows carry double padding: about 90 px each, against the mockup's 66 px.**
`TicketsEntry.tsx:379` `li.p3-row` has `padding: 12px 0` (`p3.css:88`); inside it `:381` `button.p3-date-btn` has its own `padding: 12px 0` (`p3.css:203`). 42 px of text plus 48 px of padding. Three types (Early bird / Standard / Door) plus two orders push "See all" and the orders behind the sticky footer of a 94dvh sheet that the mockup shows on one screen. P3 density rule says compact. Fix: `.p3-type .p3-date-btn { padding: 0 }`.

**D3 (should-fix, tap target). "Close" is 44 px tall but only about 40 px wide.**
`StackSheet.tsx:125-127` renders Close as `.p3-link`; `p3.css:109-112` sets `min-height: var(--tap)` but `padding: 0` and no min-width. Mockup: a 44 x 44 circle (`.x`). A thumb at the top-right misses the word and lands on the heading (nothing) or the scrim strip. `&larr; Back` is wider and passes. Fix: `min-width: var(--tap); padding: 0 8px` on the head links, or the mockup's circle.

**D4 (should-fix, clipping). 28 px money figures have about 149 px of room; four-figure Sales collide with the next tile.**
`p3.css:126` `.p3-pair` gives two 173 px columns at 390 px, minus 24 px card padding; `p3.css:128` `.p3-stat-n` is 28 px / 700 with no `overflow-wrap`; `formatPence` adds thousands separators. "£1,200.00" (a page that sold 100 x £12, an all-time total so a real shape) is roughly the full width; "£12,345.00" overflows into the Sold tile (`GetPaid.tsx:47-55`, Home tile `:238-240`). The mockup only shows "£398.00". Fix: 22 px for money, or `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, or one column when long.

**D5 (should-fix, reachability). The sheet head (Back / title / Close) is not sticky; on a long sheet Close scrolls away.**
`p3.css:170-174` `.p3-sheet` is the scroll container; `p3.css:175` `.p3-sheet-head` has no `position: sticky` while the footer has (`:234`). With four or more types, or the all-orders view, Close and Back leave the viewport; on a phone the ways out are scroll-to-top or the 6dvh scrim strip. The mockup frames never scroll so it does not show there. Fix: `position: sticky; top: -12px; background: var(--sheet)` on the head.

**D6 (nit). Switch knob stays dark when ON in light mode.** `p3.css:247` knob is `var(--fg)` with no ON override; mockup turns it white (`.sw.on::after { background: #fff }`). Contrast is fine (6.46:1); the ON state just does not read as the approved one. `var(--card)` keeps it token-only.

**D7 (nit, a11y). "Add a ticket" describes an id that is not rendered when the list is empty.** `TicketsEntry.tsx:260` `aria-describedby={why ? addId : undefined}` but `:263` only renders `#addId` when `list.length > 0`. A screen-reader user on an empty, not-live event hears a disabled button with no reason (the reason is in the empty card, `:228`, for sighted users). Fix: drop the `list.length > 0` guard or point at the empty card's text.

**D8 (nit, copy). The contributor reason is said twice in the empty sheet.** `TicketsEntry.tsx:291` (the top note) and `:228` (the empty card, where `why === NOT_MANAGER`) both say "Only an owner or manager of this page can change tickets." one card apart. Mockup says it once.

**D9 (nit, divergence). Home prompt's "Get paid" kicker is plain text, not the mockup's warn tag** (`GetPaid.tsx:212` vs mockup `.tag.warn`); **"Orders" heading is 18 px** (`TicketsEntry.tsx:250` `h4.p3-h2`, `p3.css:81`) vs the mockup's 16 px.

**D10 (nit). The loading Tickets row is a `div` with `cursor: pointer`** (`TicketsEntry.tsx:61`, `p3.css:155`). Desktop only.
