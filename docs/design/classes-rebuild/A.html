Read the beginner’s guide

Bachata class questions

Do I need a partner?

No. Most classes rotate partners every few minutes, so most people come on their own.

Which class should a complete beginner pick?

Pick one marked “Beginners slot”. Those nights list a beginners class in their programme, so you start from the basic step.

What is a class + social night?

The class comes first, then the floor opens for social dancing. You can stay and practise what you just learnt, or leave after the class.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and shoes that turn easily on the floor. You do not need dance shoes for a first class.
