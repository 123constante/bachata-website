# Going P0 &mdash; three design options (Design Gate stage 2)

Open each file on a phone or in a browser at 390px wide. Each shows the same five
screens: event page Going block (all 11 states), festival hub night rows, the
"Show me as going" switch, the dancer app "My nights" list, and the organiser view.
Numbers come from the real survey ([`../../going/shape-survey.md`](../../going/shape-survey.md)):
today **every upcoming night has 0 going**, and **4 in 5 dancer profiles are unfinished**
(usually no dance role). So the two states people will see most are "0 going" and
"finish your profile first".

| | **A &mdash; "Who&rsquo;s going" tile** ([option-a.html](option-a.html)) | **B &mdash; Button in the sticky bar** ([option-b.html](option-b.html)) | **C &mdash; Pick a night + face wall** ([option-c.html](option-c.html)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| In one sentence | A new tile on the event page holds the faces, the count and the button. | A thin faces strip near the top; "I&rsquo;m going" is the big button at the bottom of the screen. | A row of night chips (each with its count), then a grid of faces with first names, then the button. |
| Which night? | Date written at the top of the tile. | Date in the strip, small. | Very clear: you pick the night chip. Best for weekly series (38 series have 5&ndash;9 nights ahead). |
| Thumb reach | Must scroll to the tile. | Always under the thumb. | Must scroll to the tile. |
| What it pushes aside | Nothing. | The ticket button moves into its own tile (it owns the sticky bar today). | Takes the most space on the page. |
| Feel | Calm, fits the bento look. | Action-first, like a ticket shop. | Most social: you see names, not just dots. |
| Festival hub | Each night row has count, faces and its own button. | Rows show count and faces; tap opens that night. | Rows show count and faces, and the button switches in place. |
| Organiser view | Faces in a row and a "hidden" count. | Faces and "See who". | A 3-column list of names. |
| Build cost (rough) | Lowest: it reuses today&rsquo;s `RsvpBlock` tile. | Medium: the sticky bar changes on every event. | Highest: needs a night picker and per-night counts for many nights at once. |

**What is the same in all three (owner decisions):** one "I&rsquo;m going" button and no
Interested; going is for one night only; you can only say going with a finished profile
(the screen names exactly what is missing and links to it); the one switch is OFF by
default, and with it OFF you are still counted but your face never shows, not even to
organisers; ended and cancelled nights disable the button, say why and link to the next
night; "0 going" invites you to be first.

**Suggested pick if unsure:** A. It is the smallest build and the calmest look, and it
keeps the ticket button where it is. Its weak spot, "which night?", is covered by the date
line. C is worth it later if weekly series need easy night switching.

Mockups are generated by [`gen-mockups.py`](gen-mockups.py) (`python3 gen-mockups.py .`
from this folder) so all three share one set of states and data. Faces are coloured
initials, not real people.
