## Tokens / sizes (`join/JoinScreens.tsx`)
- Explicit px throughout (the phone html font-size is about 13.5px, so rem renders about 16% small): primary
  button `min-h-[48px]` 15px semibold, ghost `min-h-[44px]`, link-style controls `min-h-[44px]`, fields
  `min-h-[48px]` 16px (no iOS zoom), body 14px, helper 13px, heading 22px.
- Colours are the theme tokens only (`bg-primary`, `text-destructive`, `bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`);
  the one accent is the emerald "This is your profile" tag and the amber "Paused" tag.
- Compact rhythm (`gap-2` / `gap-3`, `p-3`), per design-density.md.
- Accessibility: labels on every field; `autocomplete` email / one-time-code / given-name; `inputmode`; focus
  moves to the step heading on every step change (`StepFrame`); errors in one `role="alert" aria-live="polite"`
  line per screen, always mounted; `motion-safe:` on the progress bar.
