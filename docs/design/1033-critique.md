# #1033 S4 front-door screens: independent design critique (Design Gate stage 5)

Fresh reviewer, not the builder. Brief `briefs/s4-critique.md` (branch `brief/s4-critique`). Judged from the
41 walk pictures in [`1033/`](1033/) at 390x844 and 360x640 (430 wide was NOT walked: the phone suite has two
projects, 390x844 and 360x640), the source in
`src/modules/admin-organisers/`, the contract [`s4-add-organiser.md`](s4-add-organiser.md), the scoped density rule,
and the rest of the admin (`/admin/organisers`, "Organiser access"). There is no approved mock, so the yardstick is
the existing admin page and the site look, and the visitor: the site admin, a beginner, on a phone, adding
organisers, sending invites, switching Run by us and clearing Needs you. Calm, plain words, one obvious next action
per screen, nothing that looks tappable but is not, every greyed control says why.

Severity: **blocker** (the visitor is misled or stuck), **should** (they hesitate or read it wrong), **nice**.
Verdict: **fixed here**, **not a bug**, or **owner call** (changes an owner decision, adds a read, or adds content).

Numbered in the order found; C18 was found by looking at the first retake. Every blocker and should is fixed here.
