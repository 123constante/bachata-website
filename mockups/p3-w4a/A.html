P3 W4a · Mockup A: You hub: rows to pushed screens

The You tab is a short list: a page card, then Switch page (only with 2+ pages), Organiser page, Team, Account. Each opens its own screen with a back link to You; every old route maps 1:1 onto a P3 route. Lowest risk; most taps.

Design only (Phase A). Every phone is 390×844. Colours come from p3.css tokens. Roles are the stored ones: Owner / Manager / Contributor. Toggle the theme to check dark.
